About

The short version of how I got here, and what I actually spend my time doing.

I work across the stack: relational databases, backend logic, and interface design, and I'm currently deepening my skills in DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and UI/UX design through AUCA's Innovation Center program, run with the Mastercard Foundation. I'm in my final year of a BSc in Information Technology at Adventist University of Central Africa, majoring in Information Management.
